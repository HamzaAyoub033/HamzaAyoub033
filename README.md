## ⭐ Featured Project: LeadFront

**[LeadFront](https://leadfront.net/) — AI Voice & Omnichannel Call Center Platform** · *Jan 2026 – Present* · [Case study](https://developerhamza.vercel.app/projects/vendira-ai-voice-platform)

A multi-tenant AI front-office platform for service businesses: real-time voice agents answer and place calls, run omnichannel campaigns, and handle scheduling, CRM and billing, all grounded in RAG-trained knowledge bases.

- 🗣️ **Real-time voice pipeline:** Deepgram (STT) → Groq / OpenAI (LLM) → ElevenLabs (TTS) over Twilio, with sub-second responses
- 🌍 **10+ languages** auto-detected mid-call
- 📡 **Omnichannel:** voice · SMS · email · chat from one dashboard
- 🧠 **RAG knowledge bases** with vector search, plus Stripe billing and Google Calendar scheduling
- 🏢 **Multi-tenant SaaS** with RBAC and PWA support
